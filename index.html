Planetary Profiles

From scorched terrestrial rocks to swirling ice giants on the edge of interstellar space.

01 • Terrestrial

Mercury

The Swift Messenger

The smallest and innermost planet in the Solar System. With almost no atmosphere to trap heat, its cratered surface swings from scorching daytime highs to freezing nights.

Day Length 59 Earth Days
Avg Temp 167°C
02 • Terrestrial

Venus

The Veiled Inferno

Similar in size to Earth, Venus is wrapped in thick clouds of sulfuric acid that trap runaway greenhouse heat—making it the hottest planet in our cosmic neighborhood.

Spin Direction Retrograde
Surface Temp 464°C
03 • Terrestrial

Earth

The Blue Oasis

Our home world and the only astronomical object known to harbor life. Liquid oceans cover 71% of its crust, shielded by a protective magnetic field and oxygen-rich sky.

Orbit Period 365.25 Days
Moons 1 (Luna)
04 • Terrestrial

Mars

The Red Frontier

Colored rusty crimson by iron oxide dust, Mars boasts Olympus Mons—the largest volcano in the Solar System—and ancient river valleys hinting at a watery past.

Tallest Peak 21.9 km
Moons 2 (Phobos & Deimos)
05 • Gas Giant

Jupiter

King of Planets

More than twice as massive as all other planets combined. Its banded clouds swirl around the Great Red Spot, a colossal anticyclonic storm raging for centuries.

Day Length 9h 55m
Known Moons 95
06 • Gas Giant

Saturn

Jewel of the Ecliptic

Adorned with a dazzling system of seven main rings composed of billions of ice and rock particles, Saturn is the only planet less dense than liquid water.

Ring Span 282,000 km
Known Moons 146
07 • Ice Giant

Uranus

The Tilted World

Rotating nearly on its side at a 98-degree axial tilt, this pale cyan ice giant experiences extreme 21-year-long seasons and the coldest planetary atmosphere in the system.

Axial Tilt 97.8°
Min Temp -224°C
08 • Ice Giant

Neptune

The Supersonic Blue

The farthest major planet from the Sun, swept by supersonic methane winds roaring faster than 2,100 km/h—the fastest sustained winds anywhere in our Solar System.

Orbit Period 165 Earth Years
Wind Speed 2,100 km/h
